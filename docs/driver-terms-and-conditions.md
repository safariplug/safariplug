# SafariPlug Driver Terms & Conditions

**Version:** driver-terms-v1-2026-09-04

> This is an operational draft for product implementation. SafariPlug should have local counsel review and approve the final legal version for each country in which drivers operate.

## 1. Eligibility and truthful information
You confirm that you are legally entitled to drive the vehicle and provide the services for which you apply. All information, declarations and documents you submit to SafariPlug must be accurate, complete, current and authentic. You must not submit another person's identity, license, registration or insurance documents or provide misleading information.

## 2. Licensing, registration and insurance
You must maintain a valid driving license for the vehicle and service class you operate, valid vehicle registration and all insurance required by applicable law. SafariPlug may require updated copies at any time. You must promptly update SafariPlug when a document is renewed, replaced, suspended, cancelled or otherwise changes.

## 3. Compliance monitoring
SafariPlug may record document issue and expiry dates and monitor them for operational and safety purposes. Automated reminders may be sent before expiry. A driver may be suspended from new assignments when a required document is missing, expired, rejected or otherwise not current. Uploading a replacement document does not by itself restore eligibility; the replacement may require review and approval.

## 4. Identity and safety verification
You agree to complete identity verification and mandatory live face/liveness verification where required. Verification is a condition of becoming eligible for assignments. SafariPlug may suspend or remove access when verification fails, expires, is revoked, or material information cannot be verified.

## 5. Vehicle standards
You are responsible for ensuring that the vehicle you use is legally registered, properly insured, roadworthy, appropriately maintained and suitable for the passenger and luggage capacity represented in your SafariPlug profile. SafariPlug may require additional vehicle inspection or evidence before activation.

## 6. Professional conduct and passenger safety
You must provide safe, lawful, respectful, reliable and professional service. You must comply with applicable traffic, licensing, passenger-safety and transport laws. You must never drive while impaired by alcohol, drugs, medication or fatigue. You must not harass, discriminate against, threaten or endanger passengers. You must promptly report accidents, safety incidents, complaints or circumstances that could affect your ability to provide safe service.

## 7. Assignments and acceptance
Assignments are subject to availability and eligibility and are not guaranteed. You may accept only assignments that you are legally and operationally able to perform. SafariPlug may assign, reassign, cancel or release a driver assignment for safety, compliance, availability, operational or customer-service reasons.

## 8. Driver substitutions
SafariPlug may substitute a driver when necessary. Where a substitution affects a customer's trip, SafariPlug may provide the customer with the verified replacement driver's name or display identity and relevant vehicle information consistent with applicable privacy and safety requirements.

## 9. Customer and company property
You must protect passenger property and return lost property through SafariPlug's designated process. You must not retain, misuse, photograph, copy or disclose passenger information except as necessary to perform the assigned service or where required by law.

## 10. Privacy and verification records
SafariPlug may collect and process driver identity, contact, license, vehicle, registration, insurance, verification, safety and operational information for onboarding, compliance, fraud prevention, safety, assignment and service operations. Private verification documents are restricted from customer-facing access. SafariPlug will process personal data according to its privacy practices and applicable data-protection law.

## 11. Suspension and termination
SafariPlug may suspend or terminate driver access where reasonably required for safety, legal compliance, document expiry, failed verification, fraud, misconduct, repeated non-performance, material complaints or breach of these terms. Suspension may prevent the driver from receiving or accepting new assignments. Existing assignments may be reassigned where necessary.

## 12. Independent responsibility
Unless a separate written agreement expressly provides otherwise, you remain responsible for your own vehicle, licensing, insurance, permits, taxes, operating costs and compliance with applicable law. Nothing in these terms by itself creates an employment relationship.

## 13. Accurate availability
You agree to keep your availability current and to accept assignments only when you are genuinely available and capable of completing them. Repeated cancellations, no-shows or inaccurate availability may affect eligibility.

## 14. Changes to these terms
SafariPlug may update these terms when its services, safety requirements, technology or legal obligations change. Where a new version requires renewed acceptance, SafariPlug may require you to review and accept the updated version before continuing to receive assignments.

## 15. Electronic acceptance
By checking the acceptance box and submitting your driver application, you confirm that you have read and agree to these Driver Terms & Conditions and authorize SafariPlug to review the information and documents submitted for onboarding, safety and compliance verification. SafariPlug records the terms version and acceptance timestamp.

## 16. Governing law and local requirements
Driver operations must comply with the laws and regulatory requirements applicable to the country and location where the service is provided. Country-specific driver requirements may supplement these general terms.
