# SafariPlug Staff Supplier Onboarding

The staff supplier command center at `/admin/suppliers` is the operational source for supplier onboarding.

Each supplier row shows:
- current onboarding stage
- completion percentage
- business contact channel
- services count
- who owns the next action (`Supplier` or `SafariPlug staff`)
- the specific next step
- direct access to Partner 360

Human governance is unchanged. Staff review is required before approval/publishing. Suppliers cannot self-verify, self-approve, self-publish, or activate bookings.
