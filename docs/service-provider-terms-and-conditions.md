# SafariPlug Service Provider Terms & Conditions

**Version:** `service-provider-terms-v2-2026-09-05`

> This provider agreement is a product/legal draft for SafariPlug. It should be reviewed by qualified local counsel before being treated as the final legal agreement in every jurisdiction where SafariPlug operates.

## 1. Platform role
SafariPlug operates a marketplace that helps customers discover and book services and experiences offered by independent service providers. Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, the provider remains responsible for delivering the booked service and SafariPlug is the marketplace/intermediary.

## 2. Provider eligibility and information
The provider must provide accurate business, contact, service, pricing, availability, licensing and professional information and keep it current. SafariPlug may request evidence of identity, business registration, professional qualifications, insurance, permits or other documents appropriate to the service.

## 3. Service quality and safety
The provider must deliver every service professionally, safely and as described. The provider is responsible for having all qualifications, licences, permits, equipment, insurance and safety procedures required for its service and location. Services involving physical activity, water sports, fitness, wellness, beauty, transport, food or other regulated/high-risk activities must comply with applicable local requirements.

## 4. Accurate listings and pricing
Providers must not publish misleading claims, unavailable services, fake reviews, false qualifications, hidden mandatory charges or materially inaccurate prices. All customer-facing mandatory charges should be disclosed before booking. Kenya's Competition Act requires consumers to be informed of charges and fees imposed for services.

## 5. SafariPlug service fee — 10% with a launch minimum
For bookings generated through SafariPlug, the provider agrees to pay SafariPlug a **10% marketplace service fee** calculated on the applicable service amount, unless a different written commercial agreement applies. For the initial Kenya service marketplace configuration, the minimum provider service fee is **KES 30 per completed booking**. SafariPlug may change the minimum or negotiate a different commercial rate for particular providers, categories or commercial programmes with appropriate notice.

The fee may be collected in either of these ways:
- deducted from the amount payable to the provider when SafariPlug processes customer payment; or
- invoiced/collected from the provider when the customer pays the provider directly, where SafariPlug permits direct collection.

**Customer booking fee:** SafariPlug's initial consumer marketplace policy is **KES 0 mandatory booking fee**. Customers should see the provider's applicable service price as the expected amount payable, unless a separate mandatory charge is clearly disclosed before payment. SafariPlug may introduce customer charges in the future only with clear advance disclosure and an updated commercial/product policy.

Providers may not misrepresent the SafariPlug service fee or use off-platform arrangements to avoid a fee on a booking that originated through SafariPlug.

## 6. Payments, settlements and taxes
Providers are responsible for their own taxes, statutory obligations, permits and accounting. SafariPlug may withhold or deduct applicable service fees, refunds, chargebacks or other amounts permitted by the applicable commercial agreement before settlement. The initial provider payout policy targets a **KES 1,000 minimum payout balance** and a weekly settlement cadence, subject to verification, payment-provider limits, reconciliation and operational controls. Settlement timing, payment methods, currency conversion and transaction fees may be specified separately by SafariPlug.

## 7. Cancellation, no-show and refunds
Providers must maintain a clear cancellation policy. They must honour confirmed bookings and must not cancel without a legitimate reason. If a provider cancels, materially changes, fails to deliver, or cannot safely provide a booked service, SafariPlug may provide the customer with an appropriate remedy, which may include rescheduling or refunding amounts collected through the platform, subject to applicable law and the provider's agreed policy.

Providers must not charge a customer a fee that was not disclosed at booking.

## 8. Availability and punctuality
Providers must keep calendars accurate and arrive prepared for confirmed appointments. Repeated late cancellations, no-shows, double bookings or inaccurate availability may result in reduced visibility, temporary suspension or removal from the marketplace.

## 9. Customer conduct and provider conduct
Providers must treat customers respectfully and may not discriminate unlawfully, harass customers, exploit customers, solicit inappropriate personal relationships, or engage in abusive, fraudulent or unsafe conduct. Customers remain subject to SafariPlug customer terms and the provider's reasonable safety rules.

## 10. Health, fitness and wellness disclosures
For fitness, sports, massage, wellness and similar services, providers must use reasonable intake/safety procedures and should advise customers when professional medical clearance is appropriate. Providers must not make unsupported medical claims or represent ordinary wellness services as medical treatment unless properly licensed to provide that treatment.

## 11. Minors and vulnerable customers
Providers must comply with applicable laws and their professional obligations when serving minors or vulnerable persons. SafariPlug may impose additional requirements for services involving children or vulnerable customers.

## 12. Reviews and authenticity
Providers must not buy, fabricate, manipulate or selectively manufacture reviews. Providers may respond professionally to reviews, but must not threaten, bribe or pressure customers to change feedback.

## 13. Customer data and privacy
Providers may use customer information received through SafariPlug only to fulfil the booking and provide legitimate customer support, unless another lawful basis or permission applies. Providers must protect customer information and must not sell it, misuse it, or add customers to marketing lists without the required permission. SafariPlug may process booking and account data according to its privacy policy and applicable data-protection law.

## 14. Intellectual property and content
The provider grants SafariPlug a non-exclusive licence to display the provider's business name, service information, photographs, logo and other submitted content for marketplace discovery, booking, marketing and related platform operations. The provider confirms it has the rights necessary to submit that content.

## 15. Verification and compliance
SafariPlug may verify provider information and may request updated documents. A provider may be required to remain verified, licensed, insured or otherwise compliant to continue offering particular services. SafariPlug may suspend or remove a service where it reasonably believes there is a safety, fraud, legal, quality or consumer-protection concern.

## 16. Off-platform bookings and fee avoidance
A provider must not intentionally redirect a customer discovered through SafariPlug away from SafariPlug for the purpose of avoiding an applicable marketplace fee, platform protection or booking record. Nothing in this clause prevents a provider from independently serving customers who did not originate through SafariPlug.

## 17. Complaints and disputes
Providers must cooperate promptly with SafariPlug in investigating customer complaints, safety incidents, payment disputes and booking discrepancies. SafariPlug may request records reasonably necessary to resolve a complaint. Where appropriate, SafariPlug may facilitate a resolution without becoming the service provider itself.

## 18. Safety incidents
Providers must promptly report serious accidents, injuries, threats, suspected fraud, safeguarding concerns or other material incidents connected with a SafariPlug booking. SafariPlug may temporarily suspend a provider while an urgent safety issue is investigated.

## 19. Suspension and termination
SafariPlug may restrict, suspend or remove a provider or service for material breach, fraud, unsafe conduct, repeated poor fulfilment, misleading information, non-payment of platform fees, unlawful activity, expired required credentials, or other material risk to customers or the platform. Where appropriate, SafariPlug will provide notice and an opportunity to resolve the issue, subject to urgent safety or legal requirements.

## 20. Independent business relationship
Unless separately agreed in writing, the provider is an independent business and not an employee, agent, franchisee or joint venturer of SafariPlug. The provider is responsible for its personnel, premises, equipment, taxes, permits and professional obligations.

## 21. Changes to these terms
SafariPlug may update these terms for legal, operational, product or commercial reasons. Material changes will be communicated through the platform or another reasonable channel. Where acceptance of a new version is required, the provider may need to re-accept the updated terms before continuing to receive new bookings.

## 22. Governing law
The applicable governing law and dispute-resolution provisions may depend on the provider's jurisdiction and SafariPlug's commercial agreement with the provider. Unless a separate agreement states otherwise, SafariPlug intends Kenya to be the primary legal framework for its Kenya marketplace operations.

## 23. Provider acceptance
By activating a SafariPlug service-provider profile and accepting these terms, the provider confirms that they have read and understood these terms, agree to the **10% SafariPlug marketplace service fee**, the applicable minimum fee, the current **KES 0 mandatory customer booking fee policy**, and the settlement rules communicated by SafariPlug, and are authorised to enter into this agreement for the provider/business.
